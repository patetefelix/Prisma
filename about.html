The name Prisma began with light. While cleaning large Restoration Hardware chandeliers, Diego saw how clean crystals catch the light and reflect it across a room. That transformation became the inspiration for Prisma: careful work that lets a space shine.

Prisma grew out of years of hands-on cleaning work in commercial spaces across the United States. From retail floors and windows to high ceilings and delicate chandeliers, that experience shaped a practical, detail-focused approach.

Today, Diego Patete brings that approach to homes, short-term rentals, and businesses in Central Florida. Based in Champions Gate, Prisma is owner-operated, with Diego personally involved in the work.

That means a direct conversation about your space, thoughtful attention to its needs, and someone who takes responsibility for the details. Whether it’s preparing for the next guest or giving a busy workplace a deeper clean, the goal is the same: a space you’re proud to welcome people into.
